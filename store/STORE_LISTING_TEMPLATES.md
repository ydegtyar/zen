# Store listing templates

Copy these values into App Store Connect / Google Play Console and adjust the contact email before submission.

## Common

- App name: Zen 101
- Subtitle / short description: A calm reader for 101 Zen stories.
- Full description:
  Zen 101 is a focused reader for the classic collection of 101 Zen stories.

  Browse short teachings, search across the collection, save favorites, and continue reading where you left off. The app supports English, Ukrainian, and Russian, with light, dark, and automatic themes for comfortable reading.

  Features:
  - Full library of 101 Zen stories
  - Full-text search by title and story content
  - Favorites for quick return
  - Continue reading and reading progress
  - Light, dark, and automatic themes
  - English, Ukrainian, and Russian localization
  - No account required
  - No advertising
